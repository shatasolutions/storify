# KPI Framework

## Product
- Time-to-first-product
- Time-to-first-paid/COD order
- Activation: publish rate
- Checkout conversion
- Refund rate / dispute rate

## Reliability/Security
- Payment webhook success rate
- Order/payment reconciliation gap
- Critical security incidents = 0

## AI
- AI suggestion accept rate
- High-risk action approval latency
- AI cost per active store

## Business
- Paid orgs
- Net revenue retention
- Pilot NPS / support ticket volume
