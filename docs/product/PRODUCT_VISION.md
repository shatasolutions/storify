# Product Vision

## Vision statement
Storify is an **AI-native, multi-tenant unified commerce platform** that lets businesses create, launch, manage, automate, and grow all sales channels from one system.

## Promise
> Build and run your entire business through one conversation.

## Beachhead
Egypt and MENA — Arabic & English first-class, RTL, regional payments, COD, WhatsApp-first commerce — with globally extensible architecture.

## What Storify is not
Single storefront demo · Shopify UI clone · Website builder only · Stripe demo · AI landing-page generator · Disconnected pages · Single-merchant app.

## Long-term commerce modes
Physical · Digital · Services · Bookings · Subscriptions · Memberships · Events · Courses · B2B · Retail POS · Social · WhatsApp · Marketplaces · Headless · Extensions · AI employees · Automation.

**Current (Verified `920c142`):** marketing shell + thin prototype (~28% / ~8%).
