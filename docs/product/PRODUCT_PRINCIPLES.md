# Product Principles

1. **Multi-tenant by default** — every commerce row is tenant-scoped.
2. **Server-authoritative commerce** — never trust client money values.
3. **Secure before clever** — rescue security before AI flash.
4. **One canonical concept** — no duplicate routes/APIs/headers/tables.
5. **MENA-first, global-ready** — AR/EN, RTL, COD, regional providers.
6. **AI with permissions** — tools, approvals, audit — not unrestricted DB.
7. **Ecosystem, not island** — integrate Shata Pay/Automation/Guard/Scraper; remain operable alone.
8. **Evidence over claims** — Definition of Done + status updates.
9. **Narrow MVP** — do not boil the ocean.
10. **Merchant time-to-value** — prompt-to-store and guided onboarding matter.
