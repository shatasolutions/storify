# Target Customers

## Primary (MVP beachhead)
- MENA merchants and social sellers (Instagram/WhatsApp-first)
- Growing brands needing bilingual storefronts + COD
- Service businesses adding product/digital commerce
- Agencies managing multiple client stores

## Secondary
- Digital product / course sellers
- Retailers preparing for POS (later)
- B2B wholesalers (later)

## Not primary initially
- Enterprise global retailers needing full Shopify parity on day one
- Pure custom headless teams (supported later via APIs)

## Jobs to be done
- Launch a store quickly with AI help
- Sell with local payment + COD
- Manage orders/inventory simply
- Operate in Arabic and English
- Automate notifications (WhatsApp)
